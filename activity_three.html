KALDERETA RECIPE

Ingredients

  1. 1 kg beef (cut into cubes)
  2. 2 tbsp cooking oil
  3. 4 cloves garlic (minced)
  4. 1 medium onion (chopped)
  5. 2 cups tomato sauce
  6. 2 tbsp tomato paste
  7. 2 cups beef broth or water
  8. 2 medium potatoes (cubed)
  9. 2 medium carrots (cubed)
  10. 1 red bell pepper (sliced)
  11. ½ cup green peas
  12. Salt and pepper to taste

Step-by-Step Procedure

  • Heat the cooking oil in a pot.
  • Sauté the garlic and onion.
  • Add the beef and cook until browned.
  • Pour in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, and beef broth.
  • Simmer until the beef is tender.
  • Add the potatoes and carrots.
  • Add the bell pepper and green peas.
  • Season with salt and pepper.
  • Cook for another 5 minutes.
  • Serve hot with steamed rice.
